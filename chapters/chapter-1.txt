Chapter 1: The Night Shift

The Lantern Archive stood at the top of Hollow Street, where the road gave up and became a staircase. Most people in Vellmar had never been inside. Mira had been inside every night for three weeks.

Her job was simple. Walk the aisles after closing, check the lamps, make sure no shelf had collapsed under the weight of a thousand forgotten books. Old Tobin, the head archivist, had given her a ring of keys and a single warning: "If a book is warm, leave it alone."

She had laughed at the time. Books were paper and glue. They were warm in summer and cold in winter, like everything else.

Tonight was the first night of winter, and the Archive was freezing. Mira could see her breath in the lamplight as she reached the end of the eastern aisle. That was when she noticed the book on the floor.

It was small, bound in faded green cloth, with no title on the spine. She picked it up without thinking.

It was warm.

Not warm like a book left near a fire. Warm like a hand that had just let go of hers.

Mira stood very still. Somewhere above her, the old clock in the tower struck midnight, and the sound rolled through the stacks like a slow wave. She looked down at the book again. The cover had not changed, but she was suddenly certain it had been waiting for her.

She should have put it back. She knew that. Instead, she tucked it under her arm, finished her rounds, and carried it home through the snow.
