Chapter 4: The Lower Vault

The lower vault was not on any map of the Archive. Tobin led her to it through a door behind the bookbinding room, down a spiral staircase so narrow that Mira had to turn sideways to fit.

At the bottom, the air was dry and still. Shelves rose into darkness on every side, and the books on them were strange. Some were bound in metal. Some were chained shut. One, on a high shelf, was breathing slowly, its cover rising and falling like a sleeping animal.

"Don't touch anything," Tobin said.

"I wasn't planning to."

The green book grew warmer in her hands as they walked. When she opened it, new words were waiting.

Left at the reading table. Third shelf from the floor. Look for the book with no shadow.

They found the reading table, an old slab of oak covered in dust. Tobin raised his lamp, and the shelf beside it lit up. Every book cast a long shadow across the floor except one, a thin volume bound in grey.

Mira pulled it free. Inside was a single page, covered in tiny handwriting. Tobin read it over her shoulder and drew a sharp breath.

"It's a binding," he said. "Whoever wrote this sealed Edda into the green book on purpose. And they left instructions for undoing it."

"Then we can save her?"

Tobin did not answer right away. He was reading the last line again, and his face had gone pale.

"We can," he said at last. "But someone has to take her place."
