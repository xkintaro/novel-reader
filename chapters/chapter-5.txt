Chapter 5: An Old Promise

They argued all the way back up the stairs.

"There has to be another way," Mira said. "Nobody should have to live inside a book."

"Edda has done it for forty years."

"That doesn't make it right."

Back in Tobin's office, Mira set the green book on the desk and opened it. If anyone deserved a say, it was Edda.

"We found the binding," she wrote on the page with Tobin's pen. "It says someone has to take your place."

The answer came slowly, as if Edda were choosing every word.

I know. I was the one who wrote it.

Mira read it aloud, and Tobin sat down heavily in his chair.

Forty years ago, a man came to the Archive with a book that was eating the town's memories. Every day, people forgot a little more of their lives. The only way to stop it was to hold it inside something stronger. So I bound it inside myself, and I bound myself inside this book. I left the grey page in case anyone ever came looking for me.

"So if we let you out," Mira said, "the thing you trapped gets out too."

Yes. Unless someone holds it.

Mira looked at Tobin. Tobin looked at the book. Outside, the snow had started falling again, soft and silent over the rooftops of Vellmar.

"I made her a promise once," Tobin said quietly. "Before she disappeared. I told her I would never let her be alone in the dark." He reached for the pen. "I think it's time I kept it."
