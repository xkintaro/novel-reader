Chapter 2: Blank Pages

Mira did not open the book until morning. She told herself it was because she was tired, but the truth was simpler. She was afraid of what she would find.

When she finally lifted the cover, she found nothing at all.

Every page was blank. Not yellowed, not stained, not torn. Just clean, empty paper, as if the book had been made yesterday and never used. She turned the pages one by one until she reached the end, then sat back in her chair and laughed at herself.

"A warm book," she said to the empty room. "Tobin will never let me hear the end of it."

She closed the book and went to make tea. When she came back, it was lying open on the table.

She was sure she had closed it. She was almost sure.

On the first page, in thin brown ink, a single line had appeared:

You took me home. Thank you. It has been very cold on the floor.

Mira dropped her cup. Tea spread across the table and soaked into the corner of the page, but the ink did not run. As she watched, a second line wrote itself beneath the first, letter by letter, as if an invisible pen were moving across the paper.

My name is Edda. I was a person once. I would like to be one again, if you will help me.

Mira read the words three times. Then she grabbed her coat, the book, and her keys, and ran back to the Archive.
